The idea

Your PC, with a wider canvas.

Air Neo is being built around a simple idea: compatible AR glasses can be more than a screen you wear. The project explores how a familiar Windows workflow could grow into a flexible spatial workspace, without requiring a separate expensive processing box.

Designed around your workflow

Less friction.
More possibility.

The goal is to make spatial desktop tools feel useful rather than overwhelming. Keep the controls clear, let the windows take focus, and make the experience approachable for everyday PC use.

Project direction

What we're working toward.

These describe the project's goals and areas of exploration. They are not a promise that every capability is already implemented in the current package.

▣

Multiple virtual screens

Explore ways to extend your desktop across a larger virtual workspace.

⌖

Spatial viewing

Investigate 3DoF-style positioning where device interfaces and software support allow.

▤

Window control

Work toward practical ways to size, place and manage desktop windows.

◉

PC-first approach

Keep your Windows computer and familiar applications at the center of the experience.

⚙

Useful customization

Build toward settings that help adapt the workspace to different needs and setups.

⌘

Open development

Follow the code, report issues and see how the project changes over time.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Is Air Neo free?

Yes. The source and project downloads are available through GitHub.

What hardware is it for?

Air Neo is intended for RayNeo AR glasses connected to a Windows PC. The RayNeo Air 3S Pro is one device used during development, but compatibility with other models is not guaranteed.

Are all the features on this page available now?

No. The visual artwork is illustrative and the feature list describes project goals. Check the README and release notes for what is actually implemented in the current build.

Is this an official RayNeo app?

No. Air Neo is an independent community project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or officially supported by RayNeo.
