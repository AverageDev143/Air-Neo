The idea behind Air Neo

Keep your setup simple.
Think beyond one screen.

Spatial computing shouldn't have to feel complicated. Air Neo is being developed as a PC-side project that explores ways to get more from compatible AR glasses, with a clean interface and an emphasis on practical desktop use.

The visual language takes cues from calm, focused desktop experiences: restrained colors, clear hierarchy, and controls that stay out of the way.

A few details

Frequently asked questions.

Is Air Neo free?

Yes. The project is free to explore and its source is available on GitHub.

Which devices is it for?

Air Neo is designed around RayNeo AR glasses connected to a Windows PC. The RayNeo Air 3S Pro has been used during development, but compatibility with other models is not guaranteed.

Are the features shown on this page finished?

No. The workspace graphic is a concept illustration. Virtual displays, spatial positioning and other items describe areas being explored, not necessarily features available in the current build. Check the README and issues for the actual project status.

Is this an official RayNeo product?

No. Air Neo is an independent community project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or officially supported by RayNeo.
