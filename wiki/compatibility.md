# Compatibility

Air Neo is an independent PC application intended to explore a spatial desktop experience with RayNeo AR glasses.

## Current target

- **Operating system:** Windows.
- **Host device:** Windows PC.
- **Glasses:** RayNeo AR glasses.
- **Device used during development:** RayNeo Air 3S Pro.

The Air 3S Pro has been used successfully during project testing. This does not guarantee that every feature works on every glasses model, Windows version, GPU or PC configuration.

## Important distinction

The project aims to explore spatial viewing, virtual screens and desktop window customization. These are development goals. Not every planned capability is implemented in the current Round 79 package.

## Before reporting a compatibility issue

Please include:

- Exact RayNeo glasses model.
- Windows edition and version.
- PC model or CPU/GPU information, if known.
- Whether the glasses work as a normal external display.
- Steps that reproduce the issue.
- Any error messages or screenshots.

## Third-party status

Air Neo is not an official RayNeo product and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or officially supported by RayNeo or InAir.
