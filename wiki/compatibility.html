Compatibility

Air Neo is a Windows PC project intended for use with RayNeo AR glasses. The RayNeo Air 3S Pro has been used during development; compatibility with other models and PC configurations is not guaranteed. Your PC's USB-C port must support DisplayPort video output for a direct USB-C glasses connection. Check the exact model, Windows version, GPU and connection method when reporting a compatibility issue. Air Neo is an independent community project, not an official RayNeo product.
