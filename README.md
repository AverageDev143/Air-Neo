# Air Neo

Have you ever bought a pair of RayNeo AR glasses and wished they supported features like 3DoF spatial tracking, virtual screens, or 3D content without having to spend $400 on an InAir Pod or use glitchy software?

If so, Air Neo is for you.

Air Neo is a free PC application that aims to bring many of those features directly to your computer. The goal is to provide an experience similar to InAir OS while being completley free.
## How It Works

RayNeo glasses, including the Air 3S Pro, already contain the sensors needed for 3DoF tracking. However, the glasses themselves do not provide the software needed to make full use of those sensors. Instead, users currently have to rely on external applications such as Airpin, Parallel or buying an external virtual processing units such as the InAir pod.

While all three options work, they each have limitations.

### Airpin

In my experience, Airpin lacks many of the features available in Parallel and it does not actually have 3DoF. It's just a shifted 2D display.

### Parallel

Parallel is much more practical and provides real 3DoF support along with support for multiple windows. However, I ran into several issues while using it:

During setup, the display is split into two sections with a blind spot in the center until all windows have been configured. Personally, it often gives me a headache.

Even after setup, some parts of the desktop feel awkward to use. For example, applications on the dock can appear on opposite sides of the display, making navigation less intuitive. I also found that opening additional applications while inside the 3D workspace isn't straightforward and often requires leaving the environment first.

### InAir pod

The  InAir pod solves all of the problems of  parallel and  more including:
- remote pc acess
- better ui
- more customization such as window distance, size etc

However the InAir pod is 400$+ which is more than the rayneo 4 pro.

## Why Air Neo Exists

Air Neo started as an attempt to make a better version of Parallel.

The goal is not just to recreate the functionality of  Parallel software, but to improve it by providing:

- Better usability
- More customization options
- Easier multitasking
- Cleaner window management
- Additional AR features over time
- A closer experience to InAir while requiring no extra hardware
