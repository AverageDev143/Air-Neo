# Air Neo
Have you bought RayNeo AR glasses and wanted features like 3DoF spatial tracking and 3D upscaling, but don't want to spend $400 on an inair Pod or deal with glitchy software?

Introducing Air Neo: a free PC-based software alternative inspired by the inair Pod experience. Air Neo brings many of the core features of inair OS to your computer, allowing you to enjoy immersive AR functionality, including spatial display features and enhanced 3D experiences, without requiring additional hardware.

# How this works:
Rayneo glasses such as the air 3s pro have the sensors for 3dof but lack the built-in software so you need to use external software such as Airpin or parallel.

While you could use Airpin or Parallel they both have their downsides :

-Airpin: In my experience it lacks many of the features  which parallel  has and its not exactly 3d its just a shifted  2d image.

-Parallel: Compared to Airpin, Parallel is more practical with actual 3dof support and multiple windows however during setup in my experience it splits the display so there is a blind spot in the middle until you finish adding your windows which gives me a headache every time but even then if you move  your cursor to the bottom of the screen your applications from your dock are on opposite sides of the  windows dock. Another issue i found  was that you cant open any other applications once your in the 3d space with out exiting  which is a big inconvenience and so my goal of Air Neo is to  fix the problems of Parallel and add more customization/ features.
